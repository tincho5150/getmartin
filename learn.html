Loss Aversion

Losses feel twice as powerful as equivalent gains. Buyers aren't irrational, they're protecting themselves. Your message needs to speak to that.

2×
losses feel twice as powerful as equivalent gains, Kahneman
60%
of no-decision losses trace directly to fear of making the wrong call: Jolt Effect

While you read this, your competitor is...

Framing their offer around what buyers stand to lose: not just what they'll gain
Reducing perceived switching risk with proof, guarantees, and phased rollouts
Turning your prospect's fear of change into a reason to move, not a reason to stall

The reframe

"Your team is losing one full day per person, per week."

vs. "Our platform could increase output by 30%." Same fact. Completely different emotional response.
